What Potluck does

See what you're owed

Your balance sits at the top, with who owes you and who you owe. Every expense shows what it did to your total.

Split it any way

Equally, by exact amounts or by percentage. Each person's amount updates as you type, and the form tells you when it adds up.

Settle up in fewer payments

Potluck works out a short list of payments that clears the whole group. Mark one as paid and every balance updates.

How it works

1

Create a group

Name it and add the people in it. It takes a few seconds and needs no account.

2

Share the link

Send the group link to your friends. Anyone with the link can see the group and add expenses.

3

Add expenses, then settle up

Log who paid for what. Potluck keeps the running totals and shows who should pay whom.

Why I built Potluck

I built Potluck for my own friend group. We were splitting trips and shared bills on Splitwise, and I wanted something simpler and more beautiful: no accounts, no ads, just a link that everyone can open.

My friends use it for real, and their feedback shapes it. Keyboard shortcuts, recording payments and the simplified settle-up plan all started as requests from them.

It's still in beta, and the code is public if you want to see how it works.
